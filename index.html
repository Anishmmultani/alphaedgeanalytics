Founder's Story

From Losing Trader
to Building the Indicator
I Always Wished Existed.

"10+ years in the markets taught me one thing — entries are easy, exits are everything."

I started my trading journey in 2016 — and like most traders, I learned the hard way. I've seen bull markets, bear markets, black swan events, and everything in between. For years my biggest struggle wasn't finding good entries — it was knowing when to exit.

I would watch winning trades turn into losses, hold too long, or exit too early. The market humbled me every time I thought I had it figured out.

After 10+ years of real market experience across NSE, NFO and MCX — I finally cracked the code. I built the RAY 2 - AlphaEdge indicator from the ground up — a multi-timeframe system using HTF, MTF and LTF levels that not only shows the best entry points but gives you a well-defined exit plan with tight stop losses, large targets, and auto-trailing SL.
